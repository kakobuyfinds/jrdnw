How to use a JoyaGoo spreadsheet

A spreadsheet organizes references; it does not reserve stock or place an order. Keep the original item link and selected variant together so you can recognize the same product at each step.

01

Find a useful reference

Filter by product type, then open the CAFANS listing. Save the product URL, item name and the exact color or style you want to compare.

02

Confirm the destination

Review the buying options on CAFANS. If you choose JoyaGoo, check that the destination shows the intended item, size, color and current price. Resolve unavailable options before paying.

03

Review before shipping

In your shopping-agent account, follow the current order instructions. Review the received item’s inspection photos, then compare parcel quotes and confirm the delivery details.

A better shortlist

What to check beyond the photo

Clothing and footwear

  • Match the selected size to that listing’s measurement chart.
  • Compare garment measurements with something you already wear.
  • For shoes, distinguish foot length from insole measurements.
  • Record the stated fabric, color, fit and included quantity.
  • Ask about missing measurements before committing.

Links, photos and order details

  • Save the original marketplace link and item identifier.
  • Keep seller photos separate from photos of received items.
  • Check inspection photos against your actual ordered variant.
  • Confirm applicable return terms and any order deadlines.
  • Recheck the price and available options before payment.

For your own comparison sheet, use these columns: product, source URL, category, selected variant, measurements, quoted price, currency, extra charges, checked date and unresolved questions. A blank answer is more useful than an assumed specification.

A little more clarity

JoyaGoo spreadsheet FAQ

What is a JoyaGoo spreadsheet?

It is a product-reference list used to organize finds for research and comparison. This page provides a small, source-linked CAFANS catalog sample and a buying checklist. It is not an official JoyaGoo product database.

Why do the main buttons open CAFANS?

CAFANS is the discovery destination for this guide. The “JoyaGoo spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons both open https://cafans.com. Product-source links open individual CAFANS listings so you can review their details and available buying options.

Are these real products from CAFANS?

The seven names, product URLs and estimated USD amounts were taken from CAFANS’s featured directory on November 19, 2026. They are a static reference sample. Changes on CAFANS do not automatically update this page.

Does the displayed price include delivery?

No. These are CAFANS homepage estimates for the products, not delivered totals. Confirm the original currency, selected option, service charges and shipping quote before deciding. Unknown charges should remain marked as unknown in your budget.

Do listing images count as QC photos?

Seller images describe a listing. Inspection or QC photos relate to a particular received item. Historical photos may help you compare visible details, but they do not verify your future order, its materials or authenticity.

What if a product link no longer works?

Return to CAFANS and search the item name or browse its category. Compare the replacement listing’s item identifier and options rather than assuming a similar title leads to the same product.

Can I complete or track an order here?

No. This page has no checkout, account access or order-tracking connection. Payments, shipping choices and support requests are handled by the service where you place your order.
